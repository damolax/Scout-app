Scope
- Preserve v10.42.2 database schema, fast direct imports, sender health, replies, signatures, permanent XP, and stale-job recovery.

Implemented
1. Failed guessed URLs no longer consume the successful-page budget.
2. Homepage-discovered contact links outrank guessed /contact paths.
3. Page budget: up to 6 successful pages, bounded by a separate attempt cap.
4. www/non-www and HTTP fallback targets are tried safely.
5. Bare words “at” and “dot” are no longer globally converted into @ and . across arbitrary page text.
6. Explicit [at]/(at)/{at} and [dot]/(dot)/{dot} obfuscation remains supported.
7. Candidate source context is captured around the exact match instead of using unrelated page-leading text.
8. Publisher/platform targets including Wikipedia, Forbes, Shopify, Medium, Quora, Crunchbase and similar sources are blocked.
9. Source evidence must belong to the saved business website before it can promote an unrelated/free mailbox.
10. Auto Scout includes Clean saved false positives.
11. The cleanup preserves legitimate same-domain and proven same-site public mailbox contacts.
12. Recent trusted saves show Why trusted and the full Source page.
